About Us

Since 2018, NordBridge has helped over 200 companies find the right people. We work across 18 countries, spanning technology, finance, healthcare, and the creative industries. Our team of specialist recruiters focuses on long-term fit rather than quick placements — we stay involved long after the offer letter is signed.

We believe that hiring is one of the most important decisions a company makes. That is why we take the time to understand not just the role, but the team, the culture, and the direction the company is heading. Every candidate we work with gets the same level of attention — we do not believe in sending out bulk applications or treating people as numbers.

Over the past seven years we have refined a process that works. It starts with a deep discovery call, moves into a carefully curated shortlist, and ends with structured onboarding support for both the candidate and the hiring manager. Our 92% one-year retention rate speaks for itself.

NordBridge is a registered recruiting agency headquartered in Vienna, Austria. All placements are subject to our standard terms and conditions. For partnership enquiries please contact our business development team directly.
